Build in “Profiling Mode”

-DGGML_HIP_EXPORT_METRICS=ON passes save temps to device compiler

RelWithDebInfo applies -O2, and -g to collect symbols



Configure:

cmake -S . -B build -DGGML_HIP=ON -DGPU_TARGETS=gfx1201 -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=RelWithDebInfo -DCMAKE_DISABLE_PRECOMPILE_HEADERS=ON -DGGML_HIP_EXPORT_METRICS=ON

Build:
cmake --build build --config RelWithDebInfo -- -j 16
